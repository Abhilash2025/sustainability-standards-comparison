CSRD Voluntary Annex

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/5011 Annex — Voluntary Standard for undertakings below 1,000 employees. Climate-related provisions extracted and mapped by module.

● Basic Module (B) · 12 Provisions

Required disclosures for all in-scope undertakings. Focuses on GHG emissions (Scope 1 + location-based Scope 2), energy consumption, and pollution.

Basic Module · GHG emissions
GHG emissions

Green house gas emissions: the text aligns more closely with global sustainability reporting standards by giving undertakings the flexibility to use either the financial control approach or the operational control approach when defining the reporting boundary to be applied.

Basic Module · GHG emissions / pollution
GHG emissions / pollution

Emission of pollutants: the text specifies that the decision on which pollutants are material for reporting purposes should be taken following a managerial assessment that considers the undertaking’s activities and sector of operation.

Basic Module · GHG emissions — Basic Module B3
GHG emissions — Basic Module B3

B3 – Energy and greenhouse gas emissions. The undertaking shall disclose its estimated total energy consumption (in megawatt-hours) and its estimated Scope 1 and location-based Scope 2 GHG emissions.

Basic Module · GHG emissions — Scope 3 considerations
GHG emissions — Scope 3 considerations

Consideration when reporting on GHG emissions under B3 (Basic Module): This includes the consideration of information on Scope 3 GHG emissions. Scope 3 emissions are indirect GHG emissions (other than Scope 2) that derive from an undertaking’s value chain.

Basic Module · scope 3 / GHG emissions
scope 3 / GHG emissions

Depending on the type of activities carried out by the undertaking, disclosing a quantification of Scope 3 GHG emissions may be relevant. Scope 3 emissions derive from an undertaking’s upstream and downstream value chain.

Basic Module · GHG emissions — Scope 1 + Scope 2
GHG emissions — Scope 1 + Scope 2

The undertaking shall disclose its estimated Scope 1 GHG emissions and its estimated location-based Scope 2 GHG emissions. [Voluntary for undertakings with 10 employees or less]

Basic Module · pollution emissions
pollution emissions

The undertaking shall disclose the emissions of pollutants to air, water and soil from its own operations.

Basic Module · GHG definitions
GHG definitions

Greenhouse Gases (GHG): The gases listed in Part 2 of Annex V to Regulation (EU) 2018/1999 of the European Parliament and of the Council.

Basic Module · scope 1 definition
scope 1 definition

Scope 1 GHG emissions: Direct GHG emissions from sources that are owned or controlled by the undertaking.

Basic Module · scope 2 definition
scope 2 definition

Scope 2 GHG emissions: Indirect emissions are a consequence of the operations of the undertaking but occur at sources owned or controlled by another entity. Location-based Scope 2 emissions: Emissions from electricity, heat, steam and cooling purchased or acquired and consumed by the undertaking.

Basic Module · scope 3 definition
scope 3 definition

Scope 3 GHG emissions: All indirect GHG emissions (not included in Scope 2 GHG emissions) that derive from an undertaking’s value chain, including both upstream and downstream emissions.

Basic Module · sustainability issues list
sustainability issues list

Appendix B list of possible sustainability issues includes: Climate Change; Climate change mitigation; Climate change adaptation; Energy; Resource use and circular economy.

● Comprehensive Module (C) · 9 Provisions

Additional disclosures for undertakings seeking deeper reporting. Covers GHG reduction targets, climate transition plans, climate risks, and sector-specific requirements for high climate-impact sectors.

Comprehensive Module · transition plans
transition plans

Climate transition plans: The text requires undertakings that report transition plans with targets that are not compatible with 1.5°C to be transparent about this.

Comprehensive Module · net-zero / climate neutrality
net-zero / climate neutrality

The Commission has also assessed the consistency of this delegated act with the climate neutrality objective set out in Article 2(1) of the European Climate Law (Regulation (EU) 2021/1119), and with the objective of ensuring progress on adaptation as referred to in Article 5 of that Regulation.

Comprehensive Module · meta-reference
meta-reference

The Commission has taken into account the technical advice provided by EFRAG... the Commission has made changes in relation to EFRAG’s technical advice as regards greenhouse gas emissions and climate transition plans.

Comprehensive Module · targets / transition — Comprehensive Module C3
targets / transition — Comprehensive Module C3

C3 – GHG reduction targets and climate transition. If the undertaking has established GHG emissions reduction targets, it shall disclose: (a) the base year and the most recent year; (b) absolute and intensity targets for Scope 1, Scope 2, and optionally Scope 3; (c) progress made towards those targets; (d) whether targets are science-based or compatible with limiting global warming to 1.5°C.

Comprehensive Module · climate risk — Comprehensive Module C4
climate risk — Comprehensive Module C4

C4 – Climate risks. If the undertaking has identified climate-related hazards and climate-related transition risks, it shall disclose: (a) a description of these risks; (b) the undertaking’s assessment of their current and anticipated financial effects; (c) adaptation measures taken or planned.

Comprehensive Module · practices — GHG reduction efforts
practices — GHG reduction efforts

(a) Practices, which may include, for instance, efforts to reduce the undertaking’s greenhouse gas emissions; (b) Future initiatives, which may include, for instance, investments or other types of climate action.

Comprehensive Module · transition plans — high climate impact sectors
transition plans — high climate impact sectors

High climate impact sectors are those listed in NACE Sections A to H and Section M. If the undertaking operates in high climate impact sectors and has adopted a transition plan, it shall describe: (a) the main features of the plan; (b) the decarbonisation levers identified; (c) the investments supporting the plan.

Comprehensive Module · climate risk — adaptation
climate risk — adaptation

Climate change adaptation: The process of adjustment to actual and expected climate change and its impacts. If the undertaking has identified climate-related hazards, it shall disclose the potential adverse effects and adaptation measures.

Comprehensive Module · net-zero benchmarks
net-zero benchmarks

This information is aligned with Article 2(1) of Regulation (EU) 2021/1119 establishing the framework for achieving climate neutrality by 2050.

Disclaimer This is a simplified analysis and does not capture all nuances of the standards. Users must refer to the actual documents for complete understanding of the details with the right context. ESRS depth is sometimes distributed across multiple disclosure requirements (e.g., governance in ESRS 2, targets in E1-6), which may understate apparent depth if viewed only through E1. CSRD Voluntary Annex is proportionate by design; low scores reflect intent, not deficiency. Timelines and effort estimates are directional, not prescriptive. National transposition of CSRD and sector-specific guidance (e.g., banks, insurers) may add requirements not captured here.
